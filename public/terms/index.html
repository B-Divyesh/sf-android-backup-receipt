Purpose and limits

Android Backup Receipt compares only the files you select. It is not a backup service, cannot access protected Android app data, and does not guarantee future availability of any destination. Before wiping a device, open representative important files and keep more than one copy of irreplaceable data.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for choosing the correct source and destination folder roots, protecting exported receipts, and confirming your backup independently. Do not use the app to inspect files you are not authorized to access.
